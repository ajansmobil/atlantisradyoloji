DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION OF X-RAY AND MAMMOGRAPHY ROOMS

Our specialised team designs, shields, and delivers turnkey X-ray and mammography suites. With 23 years of expertise, our staff of 40 physicians, 40 medical secretaries, and 5 technical specialists ensures every project meets stringent radiation safety requirements.

During the planning stage we calculate room dimensions, lead shielding thickness, ventilation, electrical infrastructure, and cooling requirements with meticulous precision. Lead shielding prevents radiation leakage beyond the room, while mammography suites incorporate tailored safety measures for low-dose imaging.

Our turnkey delivery covers construction, lead lining, electrical and HVAC works, and the installation and commissioning of X-ray and mammography systems. Working in line with national and international standards, we create safe and efficient imaging environments backed by rigorous quality control.

Each project is adapted to client needs—reinforcing existing facilities, building new rooms, or modernising legacy suites. We source durable, standards-compliant materials and prioritise radiation protection, environmental safety, and staff wellbeing throughout every stage.
