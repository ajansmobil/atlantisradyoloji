RADIOLOGY DEVICE SALES (NEW AND PRE-OWNED)

Our expert team delivers sales services for both new and pre-owned radiology devices. With 23 years of industry experience, our strong expert staff provides highly professional support. We supply MRI, CT, X-ray, mammography, and other imaging systems, offering reliable equipment at competitive prices.

Through our sales services, we match hospitals and imaging centers with MRI (Magnetic Resonance Imaging), CT (Computed Tomography), X-ray, mammography, ultrasonography, scintigraphy, and other radiology devices in both new and pre-owned options. We also deliver comprehensive consultancy on equipment selection, helping clients find quality, dependable systems that suit their budgets.

For new radiology device sales, we provide the latest technology from globally recognized brands. For pre-owned systems, we conduct detailed analyses covering technical condition, maintenance history, and calibration status to ensure the equipment we offer is trustworthy and high performing. Post-sale services include installation, calibration, and staff training.

We develop tailored solutions aligned with the needs of each customer, offering support with financing options, trade-ins, and evaluation of existing devices. Long-term assistance after the sale covers maintenance, repairs, and spare parts supply. With 23 years of expertise, we remain a trusted partner in radiology device sales.
