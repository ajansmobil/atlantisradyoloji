DOPPLER AND ULTRASONOGRAPHY SERVICE

Within our Doppler and ultrasonography imaging services, we provide high-quality diagnostic imaging and reporting with our specialist radiology team. Backed by 23 years of experience, our strong expert staff delivers professional services. Doppler ultrasonography is a dedicated technique used to evaluate blood flow.

Ultrasonography visualises internal body structures through sound waves. Because it involves no radiation, it is a safe imaging method—even during pregnancy. Doppler ultrasonography is an advanced approach that measures the direction, velocity, and characteristics of blood flow, helping to detect vascular blockages, narrowings, and other abnormalities.

Using PACS software, we transfer Doppler and ultrasonography studies performed in the hospitals we serve and deliver teleradiology reports online from anywhere in the world. We operate 24/7 without distinction between cities or countries, and in urgent cases we deliver reports within one hour to any destination.

Our Doppler and ultrasonography portfolio covers a broad range: assessment of abdominal organs such as the liver, kidneys, gallbladder, pancreas, and spleen; examinations of superficial organs including the thyroid, breast, and prostate; cardiac echocardiography; prenatal monitoring; and vascular disease evaluation. Our expert radiologists review every Doppler and ultrasonography study to the highest quality standards and prepare comprehensive reports.
