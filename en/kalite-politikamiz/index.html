ATLANTIS RADIOLOGY QUALITY POLICY

Atlantis Radiology's quality policy is to ensure error-free reporting through processes aligned with national legislation and international standards, enhance service quality via regular audits and training, and safeguard patient safety and information security at every stage.

We keep team competencies up to date with continuous training programmes, reinforce our PACS infrastructure and reporting platforms through scheduled maintenance, monitor quality targets with measurable performance indicators and internal audit reports, and evaluate feedback systematically.

Information security, patient privacy and adherence to ethical values are the foundations of our quality management. We comply with KVKK and relevant healthcare regulations at every service step, and operate with an approach that considers energy efficiency and environmental impact.
