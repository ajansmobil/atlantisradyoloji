CONSULTANCY SERVICES

We deliver expert consultancy services in radiology. With 23 years of experience and knowledge, our strong team of 40 physicians, 40 medical secretaries, and 5 technical specialists provides professional guidance on equipment selection, project planning, licensing, and technical support. We create tailored solutions for every need in the radiology sector.

Within our consultancy scope, we offer comprehensive services for establishing radiology departments in hospitals and imaging centers, modernizing existing units, procuring new equipment, selecting technical hardware, and training personnel. Consultancy for obtaining TAEK (Turkish Atomic Energy Authority) licenses for radiology devices is also among our core services.

In our equipment selection consultancy, we perform detailed analyses to choose MRI, CT, X-ray, mammography, and ultrasonography devices that match the specific needs of the healthcare facility. Through our project planning consultancy, we provide expert support on designing radiology rooms, calculating lead shielding, planning magnetic shielding, arranging electrical installations, and configuring ventilation systems. Our licensing consultancy guides clients throughout the TAEK license application process.

Our technical support services cover installation, calibration, maintenance, and repair of radiology devices with the help of our specialist team. We also organize comprehensive training programs for radiology technicians, including device operation and safety courses. Leveraging 23 years of experience, we deliver professional solutions for every requirement in the radiology sector.
