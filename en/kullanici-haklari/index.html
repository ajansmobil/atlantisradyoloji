User Rights

Institutions and individuals who benefit from Atlantis Radiology services have the right to access, correct, delete, object to the processing of their personal data, and request up-to-date information. You can submit your requests in writing or via our registered e-mail channels and receive transparent feedback on every step.

Your applications are evaluated and finalised within the periods defined by KVKK. We protect your data through the identity verification steps in our request forms, create a record for each application and keep you informed throughout the process.

To exercise your rights or obtain detailed information, please use the communication channels listed in our privacy policy. Our team receives regular training on user rights and resolves every request in accordance with the legislation.
