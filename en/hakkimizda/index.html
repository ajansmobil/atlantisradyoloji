ABOUT ATLANTIS RADIOLOGY

ATLANTIS RADIOLOGY IMAGING HEALTH SERVICES LTD. was founded to meet Turkey's growing need for sharing and archiving diagnostic imaging studies and to deliver the most accurate diagnostic reports through our experienced radiologists and reporting specialists. Using globally adopted PACS platforms, we securely transfer and report MRI, MR angiography, CT, CT angiography, mammography, digital X-ray, and scintigraphy studies performed at the hospitals we serve, enabling teleradiology reporting from anywhere worldwide.

Contemporary medicine increasingly focuses on patient-specific data, which calls for subspecialised radiologists to interpret imaging acquired by advanced modalities. Diagnostic images captured or transmitted from anywhere in the world are routed to our PACS infrastructure for reporting. With 23 years of experience, our strong team of physicians, medical secretaries, and technical staff delivers highly professional services to the sector.

To date we have produced over 10,000,000 reports and currently generate around 300,000 reports per month, continually expanding our network by onboarding new hospitals. Recognising the sector's potential for development, Atlantis Health Services relentlessly invests in innovative software and hardware to stay ahead of industry needs. We provide reporting services 24/7, including nights, weekends, and public holidays, without distinction of city or country.
