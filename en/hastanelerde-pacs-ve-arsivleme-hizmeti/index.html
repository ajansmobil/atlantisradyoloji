PACS AND ARCHIVING SERVICES IN HOSPITALS

A PACS (Picture Archiving and Communication System) manages the capture, archiving, and distribution of radiological images in digital form. In recent years, advances in medical technology have progressed in parallel with developments in computing, making it easier to transport and store diagnostic images and reports.

Beyond secure storage, PACS enables images to be delivered to any workstation within the healthcare facility—or to external locations—via computer networks. A DICOM-compliant infrastructure is required to transmit images, ensuring that archived studies can be retrieved instantly from thousands of patient records whenever needed.

Reusing previously acquired images rather than re-imaging patients streamlines treatment and reduces radiation exposure. Historical reports and images can be compared to monitor disease progression, while digital storage prevents image degradation and allows output at original quality whenever prints are required.

Hospitals and imaging centres can archive as many studies as needed through our central PACS service, avoiding costly on-site investments. Leveraging internet connectivity, the system is accessible worldwide without additional infrastructure. Institutions can engage our services on weekly, monthly, or annual agreements, and all images and reports transmitted for remote reporting are securely stored within the system.
