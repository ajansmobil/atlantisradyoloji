DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION OF COMPUTED TOMOGRAPHY ROOMS

Computed Tomography (CT) rooms require specialized radiation protection standards as they utilize X-ray-based cross-sectional imaging technology. With 23 years of industry experience, we provide expert services for CT room design, lead shielding, and turnkey construction. Since CT systems generate high-dose X-rays, radiation isolation of these rooms is of critical importance.

In CT room planning, lead equivalent (Pb eq) calculations are performed based on the device's kV value (typically 80-140 kV) and mAs settings. Standard CT rooms generally require 2-3 mm lead equivalent, while high-power systems may need 4-5 mm. Wall, ceiling, and floor shielding utilize lead sheets, leaded concrete, or special plasters containing barium sulfate. A separate control room design with leaded glass windows is mandatory for the operator console.

CT systems generate significant heat, making cooling systems critical. Rooms must be at least 25-30 m² with ceiling heights of 2.8-3.2 m. Electrical infrastructure requires 380V/3-phase power supply, UPS systems, and proper grounding. Ventilation must provide at least 6-8 air changes per hour. Water cooling units for contrast injection and patient preparation areas are also integrated into the design.

In our turnkey CT room projects, we also manage TAEK (Turkish Atomic Energy Authority) licensing and radiation safety reporting processes. Post-installation radiation measurement tests are conducted and certification is provided. We are experienced in modernizing existing hospital CT rooms and adapting them for new-generation multislice systems. All projects comply with NCRP (National Council on Radiation Protection) and ICRP (International Commission on Radiological Protection) standards.
