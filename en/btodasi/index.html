DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION OF COMPUTED TOMOGRAPHY ROOMS

We provide specialized services for the design, lead shielding, and turnkey construction of Computed Tomography (CT) rooms. With 23 years of experience, our strong team of 40 physicians, 40 medical secretaries, and 5 technical specialists delivers highly professional expertise in the sector. Designing CT rooms is a process of critical importance for radiation safety.

During CT room design, technical details such as room dimensions, lead shielding thickness, ventilation systems, electrical infrastructure, and cooling units are carefully calculated. Lead shielding is the most essential safety measure to prevent radiation from spreading outside the room. The thickness of the lead lining is determined according to the power of the CT device and applied in full compliance with standards.

In our turnkey CT room service, we manage every stage from project planning to construction, lead shielding, electrical installation, ventilation, cooling, installation of the CT system, and commissioning. Our expert team works in line with national and international standards to deliver safe and efficient CT suites. We rigorously apply quality control processes in all projects, keeping customer satisfaction at the forefront.

Throughout the design and construction of CT rooms, we develop tailored solutions according to the needs of our clients. Our experienced specialists support projects such as strengthening existing facilities, building new CT rooms, and renovating outdated suites. The materials we use comply with national and international standards and are durable and high quality. In every CT room project, radiation safety, environmental protection, and personnel security are our top priorities.
