Processing of Personal Data

Your personal data is processed by Atlantis Radiology under Law No. 6698 (KVKK) to conduct diagnostic and reporting activities, measure service quality and fulfil legal obligations. Data is shared only with relevant parties and legally required authorities, and is anonymised when necessary.

During data retention we record access logs, apply role-based authorisation and encrypted storage methods. When statutory periods expire, your information is securely destroyed, and the same security standards apply to all backup processes.

You may request information about data processing activities at any time, or ask for your data to be corrected, deleted or object to its processing. Application forms and contact details are provided within our KVKK request procedure.
