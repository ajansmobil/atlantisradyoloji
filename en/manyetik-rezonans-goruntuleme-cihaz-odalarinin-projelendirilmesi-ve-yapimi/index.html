DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION OF MAGNETIC RESONANCE IMAGING SUITES

Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MR) rooms operate using magnetic fields and radiofrequency waves without radiation. Therefore, MR rooms require magnetic field isolation and RF (Radio Frequency) shielding rather than radiation protection. With 23 years of experience, we provide expert services for MR room design and turnkey construction. MR systems generate powerful magnetic fields ranging from 0.5 Tesla to 7 Tesla.

In MR room design, magnetic field shielding (Faraday cage) is the most critical component. RF shields made of stainless steel or copper mesh cover all room surfaces (walls, ceiling, floor) with electrical continuity ensured. While 1.5 Tesla systems typically require 2-3 mm stainless steel, higher field systems (3 Tesla and above) need thicker shielding. Non-ferromagnetic construction materials are used to prevent magnetic field leakage.

Since MR systems use superconducting magnets, continuous cryogen cooling is required. Oxygen monitors and ventilation systems are mandatory against helium leaks. Rooms must be at least 40-50 m² with ceiling heights of 3.5-4 m. Electrical infrastructure uses fiber optic cables unaffected by magnetic fields. Acoustic insulation is critical as MR systems generate 80-120 decibels of noise. Special acoustic panels and insulation materials are applied for patient comfort.

In our turnkey MR room projects, field mapping tests are conducted and the 5 Gauss line (magnetic field safety boundary) is determined. Outside this boundary, magnetic devices such as pacemakers and metal implants can be safely used. For MR room installation in existing buildings, structural analysis is performed and the impact of magnetic materials such as elevators and iron columns is calculated. All projects comply with IEC (International Electrotechnical Commission) and FDA (Food and Drug Administration) magnetic field safety standards.
