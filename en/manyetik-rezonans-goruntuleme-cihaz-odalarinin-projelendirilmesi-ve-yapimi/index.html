DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION OF MAGNETIC RESONANCE IMAGING SUITES

We deliver specialised design and turnkey construction services for Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MR) suites. With 23 years of industry experience, our strong team of 40 physicians, 40 medical secretaries, and 5 technical experts manages projects that demand the highest standards of magnetic safety.

During the design phase we meticulously calculate room dimensions, magnetic shielding, ventilation, electrical infrastructure, cooling, and acoustic insulation. Magnetic shielding is critical for preventing the MR system's strong magnetic field from extending beyond the room. Shielding thickness is determined according to the magnet's Tesla rating and implemented in full compliance with regulations.

Our turnkey delivery covers every stage—from concept and construction to magnetic shielding, electrical works, HVAC, acoustic treatments, system installation, and commissioning. Working to national and international standards, our experts build MR suites that are safe, efficient, and reliable. We maintain rigorous quality-control processes to ensure consistent client satisfaction.

We tailor each MR suite project to the customer's requirements, whether strengthening existing facilities, building new rooms, or modernising legacy installations. All materials conform to national and international standards and are selected for durability and performance. Magnetic field safety, environmental stewardship, and staff protection are the core priorities in every MR project we deliver.
