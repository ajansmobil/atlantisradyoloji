Our Privacy Policy

At Atlantis Radiology we process all images and report data received from our partner healthcare institutions under strict confidentiality, limit access to authorised personnel, transmit data through encrypted channels and comply fully with KVKK and all related regulations.

We regularly monitor access logs across our digital systems, retain cookie and session data only for as long as necessary, and require data privacy provisions in agreements with third-party service providers. Where needed, we apply anonymisation methods. Our users can update their cookie preferences at any time and request detailed information about how their personal data is used.

You may contact our KVKK officers with any questions about our privacy policy, and transparently learn how your information is protected and which security controls are applied.
