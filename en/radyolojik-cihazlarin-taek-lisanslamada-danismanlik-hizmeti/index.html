CONSULTANCY FOR TAEK LICENSING OF RADIOLOGICAL DEVICES

Our specialist team provides consultancy for obtaining TAEK (Turkish Atomic Energy Authority) licenses for radiological devices. With 23 years of experience, our strong staff of 40 physicians, 40 medical secretaries, and 5 technical specialists offers professional guidance throughout licensing processes and regulatory compliance.

Within our TAEK licensing consultancy, we prepare all required documentation, manage application procedures, coordinate communication with TAEK, and handle license renewal processes. We deliver expert support for licensing MRI, CT, X-ray, mammography, scintigraphy, and other radiological systems.

We provide detailed guidance on defining device technical specifications, meeting safety standards, implementing environmental protection measures, and delivering personnel safety training. Our experts assist in resolving technical and legal challenges encountered during licensing, ensuring each step complies with TAEK regulations.

Tailoring our consultancy to the needs of hospitals and imaging centers, we plan licensing roadmaps for new equipment acquisitions, manage renewals for existing systems, and address issues that arise during regulatory processes. Backed by 23 years of experience, we consistently achieve successful outcomes in TAEK licensing projects.
