MAINTENANCE, REPAIR, AND SPARE PARTS SUPPLY FOR RADIOLOGICAL DEVICES

We deliver expert maintenance, repair, and spare parts supply services for radiological devices. With 23 years of experience, our strong team of 40 physicians, 40 medical secretaries, and 5 technical specialists offers highly professional support. By providing 24/7 technical assistance and rapid service, we keep your systems running without interruption.

Our maintenance services cover periodic servicing, calibration, and safety checks for all radiological equipment, including MRI, CT, X-ray, mammography, ultrasonography, and scintigraphy systems. With our experienced technicians, we maintain and repair every brand and model of radiology device, applying tailored maintenance programs that extend equipment lifespan and enhance performance.

Within our repair services, we handle fault diagnostics, repair planning, spare parts procurement, repair operations, and post-service testing and calibration swiftly and reliably. Our 24/7 technical support line ensures quick intervention in emergencies, and we provide dedicated service programs to keep hospital and imaging center equipment continuously operational.

For spare parts supply, we source a broad range of original, compatible, and custom-manufactured components. We maintain inventory for all brands and models of radiology devices, offering fast delivery and competitive pricing. With 23 years of expertise, we are your trusted partner for radiological equipment maintenance, repair, and spare parts supply.
