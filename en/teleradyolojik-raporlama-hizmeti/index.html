TELERADIOLOGY REPORTING SERVICE

ATLANTIS RADIOLOGY IMAGING HEALTH SERVICES LTD. was established to meet Turkey's needs for sharing and archiving diagnostic imaging studies and to deliver accurate reports through our experienced radiologists and reporting specialists. We leverage globally recognised PACS platforms to transfer and report MRI, MR angiography, CT, CT angiography, mammography, digital X-ray, and scintigraphy examinations performed in partner hospitals, enabling teleradiology from anywhere in the world.

Remote reporting allows subspecialist radiologists to interpret studies outside the original acquisition site—whether in another city or country—and deliver expert reports. Widely adopted abroad for many years, this model has been successfully implemented in Turkey by our company, with turnaround times improving in parallel with technological advancements.

In locations without onsite radiologists, especially emergency departments, remote reporting can be lifesaving. It offers a convenient and cost-effective solution for facilities lacking sufficient specialists or requiring expert second opinions. We provide reporting services around the clock—evenings, weekends, and public holidays included—and deliver urgent reports within one hour, anywhere in the world.

With 23 years of experience, our robust team comprises 40 physicians, 40 medical secretaries, and 5 technical experts. We have completed 5,000,000 reports to date, averaging 150,000 reports per month, and continue to expand by partnering with new hospitals.
